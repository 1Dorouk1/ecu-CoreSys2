Welcome

The Faculty of Economics and International Trade encourages its students to develop the required skills and knowledge to engage in the business world as qualified employees and business leaders with creative and innovative ideas.

The faculty also enhances entrepreneurship by inspiring students to participate in entrepreneurial communities, competitions and real business experiences.

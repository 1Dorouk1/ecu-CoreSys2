Faculty of Physical Therapy

The only faculty in the Middle East and worldwide that integrates Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM) with Physical Therapy education.

Welcome

Faculty of Physical Therapy at ECU is the only faculty that integrates Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM) into the physical therapy curriculum.

We are pioneers in combining modern physical therapy sciences with traditional and advanced Chinese medicine practices.

Vision

The faculty aspires to achieve excellence locally, regionally, and internationally in integrating physical therapy with Chinese medicine through innovative education, advanced research, and community services.

Mission

The faculty is committed to preparing highly qualified graduates capable of competing globally through an integrated academic program combining physical therapy and Chinese medicine.

Program Mission

The Physical Therapy Program prepares distinguished graduates through advanced education, research support, and community services within professional ethics.

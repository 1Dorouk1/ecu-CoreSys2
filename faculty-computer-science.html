Welcome

Welcome to the Faculty of Computers and Information Systems at ECU University, a dynamic hub for education, research, and innovation in computing and information technology.

Vision

The faculty aspires to be a leading institution in computing and information technology education, research, and innovation, producing highly skilled graduates who meet global job market demands.

Mission

The faculty provides high-quality education and hands-on training in computing and information systems, developing students’ technical expertise, problem-solving abilities and research skills.

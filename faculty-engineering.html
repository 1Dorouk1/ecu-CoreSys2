Faculty of Engineering and Technology

The Egyptian Chinese University offers high-demand, practice-oriented study programs following international standards in education.

Welcome

The Egyptian Chinese University ECU is the only Chinese university in Egypt and the Middle East. The ECU offers high-demand study programs and non-traditional fields of study.

ECU implements international standards in education and follows a practice-oriented teaching strategy. About 50% of the study load is in practical courses, giving ECU students the skills they need in any working environment.
